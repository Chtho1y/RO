Description:
Image Atlasser is a tool that uses the most efficient image packing method, out of several ones (can be overriden to manual selection).

The generated Atlas is a PNG image imported as Sprite texture that auto-assigns your selected texture parts. It can also extract your packed textures from the very own atlas (using the exported codings) or your custom atlas (using sprite meta data!).
